Beyond the Solar System

Cosmic Wonders

Illustrations drawn with pure CSS, inspired by some of the most extreme objects known.

Black Holes

Regions where gravity is so strong that not even light escapes. The first image of one, in the galaxy M87, was released in April 2019.

Nebulae

Giant clouds of gas and dust where new stars are born. The Pillars of Creation sit about 6,500 light-years away in the Eagle Nebula.

Galaxies

Islands of billions of stars. The Andromeda Galaxy is about 2.5 million light-years away and is heading toward our Milky Way.

Pulsars

Fast-spinning neutron stars that sweep beams of radiation across space. A teaspoon of their material would weigh billions of tons.
